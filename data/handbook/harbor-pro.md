# Harbor Pro Programme

Harbor Pro is our membership for tradespeople and serious hobbyists. It costs $99 a year in the United States, €95 in the European Union and £85 in the United Kingdom.

## Benefits

Members get free express shipping on every order, with no minimum. They pay no restocking fee on returned power tools in any region. A dedicated phone line, open seven days a week, connects them to senior support staff.

Members also get early access to new tools, usually two weeks before general release, and an extended warranty. The extension adds two years to the standard coverage of every power tool bought while the membership is active.

## Eligibility

Anyone over 18 can join. Businesses can buy up to five linked memberships under one account, and each linked member gets the full set of benefits.

## Cancellation

Members can cancel at any time. If they cancel within 30 days of joining and have not used the free express shipping, they get a full refund. After that, the membership stays active until the end of the paid year and is not renewed.

Warranty extensions already granted remain valid after cancellation. They apply to tools bought while the membership was active.
