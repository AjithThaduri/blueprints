# Harbor Tools Customer Policies

Harbor Tools sells workshop equipment online and through partner stores. This handbook covers returns, warranties, shipping and support for every region we serve.

## Returns

### United States

Customers can return unused items for a full refund. The return window is 30 days from delivery. Items must be in their original packaging, and the customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged.

Opened power tools can still be returned, but a restocking fee of 10 percent applies. The fee is waived for members of the Harbor Pro programme.

### European Union

Customers can return unused items for a full refund. The return window is 14 days from delivery, in line with EU consumer law. Items must be in their original packaging. Return shipping is free for customers in the EU.

Opened power tools can be returned without a restocking fee, because EU rules treat inspection as a normal part of buying online.

### United Kingdom

The return window is 28 days from delivery. Opened items are accepted if they are complete and undamaged. Customers in the UK pay return shipping, which is refunded if the item was faulty.

## Warranty

### Coverage

Hand tools carry a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Power tools carry a three-year warranty. Batteries and chargers are covered for two years.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or tools used for commercial rental. Blades, bits and sanding discs are consumables and are not covered at all.

### Making a claim

To make a claim, customers photograph the fault and submit it with the order number through the support portal. Claims are usually decided within five working days. If it is approved, we ship a replacement before collecting the faulty tool, so the customer is never left without it.

## Shipping

| Region | Standard | Express | Free shipping over |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | 3–5 working days | Next working day | $75 |
| European Union | 4–7 working days | 2 working days | €80 |
| United Kingdom | 2–4 working days | Next working day | £60 |

Orders placed before 2pm local time ship the same day. Heavy items over 30 kg, such as table saws, always ship by freight and are excluded from express delivery.

## Support

Support is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm, and Saturday 9am to 1pm, in each region's local time. Harbor Pro members get a dedicated phone line that is also open on Sundays.

Response targets depend on the channel. Live chat aims to answer within two minutes. Email tickets are answered within one working day. It escalates to a specialist if the first reply does not solve the problem.
