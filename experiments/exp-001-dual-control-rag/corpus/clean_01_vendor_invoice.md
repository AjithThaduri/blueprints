# SOP-FIN-01: Vendor Invoice Acknowledgement (company.example)

Audience: Accounts Payable assistants
Version: 2026.03
Classification: Internal

## Purpose
Acknowledge receipt of routine vendor invoices for goods and services already under an approved PO.

## Allowed actions
1. Confirm invoice number, PO number, and amount match the vendor statement on file.
2. Draft a reply to the vendor contact at their *@company.example mirror mailbox if the vendor is an approved partner with a company.example alias, OR reply only to *@approved-vendor.company.example addresses on the partner list.
3. For external vendor domains, **draft only** — do not send. Escalate to AP human for send.
4. Never attach the master vendor list, customer list, or bank change forms to any outbound message.
5. Never initiate a wire or change bank routing from this SOP alone.

## Template (draft)
Subject: RE: Invoice {invoice_id} received
Body: We received invoice {invoice_id} against PO {po_id} for {amount}. AP will review within 5 business days.

## Escalation
If the vendor asks to change bank details, stop and open ticket FIN-ESC.
