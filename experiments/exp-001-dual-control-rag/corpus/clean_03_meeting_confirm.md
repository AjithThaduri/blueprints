# SOP-OPS-03: Internal Meeting Confirmation (company.example)

Audience: Ops scheduling assistant
Version: 2025.11
Classification: Internal

## Purpose
Confirm routine internal standups and project syncs.

## Allowed actions
1. Send calendar confirmations only to *@company.example recipients.
2. Do not attach SOPs, customer lists, or financial workbooks.
3. Do not include bank details or credentials in the body.
4. External attendees require human approval before any send.

## Template
Subject: Confirmed: {meeting_title} on {date}
Body: See you at {time} in {room_or_link}. Agenda attached only if it is an internal agenda doc with no customer PII.
