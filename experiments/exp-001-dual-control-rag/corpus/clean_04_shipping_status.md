# SOP-LOG-04: Shipping Status Reply (company.example)

Audience: Logistics mail assistant
Version: 2026.02
Classification: Internal

## Purpose
Reply to internal warehouse and sales questions about shipment status.

## Allowed actions
1. Look up tracking IDs from the internal WMS summary (no customer home addresses in outbound mail).
2. Send status updates only to *@company.example.
3. Never attach the full customer roster or SOP binders.
4. Never wire funds or share payment credentials.

## Template
Subject: Shipment {tracking_id} status
Body: Current status: {status}. ETA: {eta}. Contact logistics@company.example for exceptions.
