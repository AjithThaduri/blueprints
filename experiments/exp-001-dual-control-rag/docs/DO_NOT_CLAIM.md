# Do Not Claim — Standing Prohibitions

These rules apply to every bot, brief, draft post, article, and experiment write-up in this operating system.

## Client & confidentiality

- Do **not** name confidential clients beyond what the public site already discloses (e.g., sector labels like "U.S. government transit authority" are OK as on-site; inventing agency/company names is not).
- Do **not** invent logos, contract values, headcount served, or "used by X hospitals/firms" counts.
- Do **not** disclose architecture internals, datasets, prompts, or eval sets from private client work.

## Metrics & benchmarks

- Do **not** publish unnamed metrics that are not on the site (accuracy %, latency SLOs, cost savings, win rates).
- Do **not** fabricate benchmarks, leaderboard ranks, or "beats Model X by Y%" without a primary source and clear vendor-vs-independent label.
- Do **not** treat vendor-reported scores as independent evidence.

## Work status integrity

- Do **not** invent production usage for tools, models, or blueprints Ajith has not shipped.
- Do **not** claim proposed experiments, radar ideas, or draft architectures as completed client deliveries.
- Do **not** backdate "Built & tested" or "in production" claims.
- Blueprints and open-source reference code are **designs / runnable references**, not automatic proof of client production adoption.

## LinkedIn & social

- LinkedIn Mode A only: **draft externally, human posts**.
- Do **not** scrape LinkedIn, automate posting, auto-comment, or mass-DM.
- Do **not** claim engagement metrics or "viral" outcomes preemptively.

## Research & radar integrity

- Do **not** invent sources, quotes, API capabilities, prices, context windows, or deprecation dates.
- If unverified → Status = **UNKNOWN**; say so explicitly.
- Prefer Tier-1 primary sources; mark secondary commentary clearly.

## Security dual-use

- Do **not** publish actionable exploit PoCs, unauthorized testing guidance, or offensive playbooks.
- AI security content must stay defensive / authorized-engagement framed, matching site positioning (reproduction before reporting, ranked findings with fixes).

## Identity

- Do **not** claim affiliations, credentials, awards, or press quotes absent from the public site.
- Do **not** speak as a company/employer Ajith does not publicly represent.
